Andromeda Galaxy (M31)

The nearest spiral galaxy to the Milky Way, it is on a collision course with our galaxy.

Triangulum Galaxy (M33)

A spiral galaxy and a member of the Local Group, it’s smaller than both the Milky Way and Andromeda.

Sombrero Galaxy (M104)

Known for its bright nucleus and dust lane, this spiral galaxy is located in the constellation Virgo.

Whirlpool Galaxy (M51)

A classic spiral galaxy interacting with a smaller galaxy, it’s known for its beautiful spiral arms.
