Helix Nebula

nearest nebula from earth

Orion Nebula (M42)

A bright and easily visible nebula in the Orion constellation, it's a stellar nursery where new stars are being formed.

Eagle Nebula (M16)

Famous for the "Pillars of Creation" image, this region is another stellar nursery located in the constellation Serpens.

Ring Nebula (M57)

A well-known planetary nebula in the constellation Lyra, characterized by its ring-like appearance.

Horsehead Nebula

A dark nebula located in the constellation Orion, it resembles the head of a horse and is a prominent feature in many images of the region.

Dumbbell Nebula (M27)

A planetary nebula in the constellation Vulpecula, it's one of the brightest nebulae and is shaped like a dumbbell.
