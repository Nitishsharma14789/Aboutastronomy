Calvera

nearest neutron star to earth

PSR B1919+21

The first pulsar discovered, it emits regular pulses of radiation and was detected in 1967 by Jocelyn Bell Burnell and Antony Hewish.

PSR J0737-3039

A binary pulsar system, it provides insights into gravitational waves and has helped test theories of gravity.

PSR B0656+14

Known for its relatively strong magnetic field, this pulsar emits X-rays as well as radio waves.

Vela Pulsar (PSR B0833-45)

Located in the Vela constellation, it’s a young pulsar known for its bright pulses and X-ray emissions.
