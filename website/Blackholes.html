Black of milkyway

M87*

Located in the galaxy M87, this supermassive black hole gained fame after the first-ever image of a black hole was captured by the Event Horizon Telescope.

LIGO Black Holes

The first detected black holes from gravitational waves by LIGO (Laser Interferometer Gravitational-Wave Observatory) were in binary systems, including those in the event GW150914.

GW190521

A black hole merger detected by LIGO and Virgo, creating one of the most massive black holes known, estimated at about 142 solar masses.

The Phoenix Cluster Black Hole

A supermassive black hole at the center of the Phoenix Cluster, one of the most massive galaxy clusters known.
